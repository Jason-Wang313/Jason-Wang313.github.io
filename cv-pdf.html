Research Direction

I have moved from earlier work in AI safety, alignment, benchmark design, and theoretical ML toward a new research agenda centered on physical world models and embodied intelligence. My long-term goal is to build adaptive physical world models that compose known mechanisms, identify missing physical concepts, actively gather evidence, and continually update for robust open-world robot generalization.
